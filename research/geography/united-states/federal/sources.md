# United States state-and-local tax source record

Last verified: 2026-08-01.

- Property tax design and administration are primarily state and local matters;
  comparable national revenue data come from the Census Bureau's government
  finance surveys. Source: [Annual Survey of State and Local Government Finances](https://www.census.gov/programs-surveys/gov-finances.html)
  and [Quarterly Tax Survey](https://www.census.gov/econ/overview/go0600.html).
- For federal tax year 2026, the overall individual SALT deduction limit is
  $40,400 ($20,200 married filing separately), with an income-based reduction
  above specified modified-AGI thresholds but not below $10,000 ($5,000 MFS).
  This applies only to taxpayers who itemize and is not a credit against the local
  bill. Source: [IRS 2026 Form 1040-ES correction](https://www.irs.gov/forms-pubs/correction-to-state-and-local-income-tax-deduction-amount-in-the-2026-form-1040-es).

## Comparison rule

Cross-state comparisons must separate assessment ratio, taxable value, exemption,
rate, levy, special-district charge, and payment timing. A headline rate alone is
not a comparable household burden.
