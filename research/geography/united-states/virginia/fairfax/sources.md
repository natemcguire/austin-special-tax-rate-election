# Fairfax County source record

Last verified: 2026-08-01. Rates are dollars per $100 of assessed value.

- The Board of Supervisors adopted the FY 2027 budget on May 5, 2026, effective
  July 1, and reduced the base real-estate rate from $1.1225 to $1.12. Source:
  [Fairfax County adopted-budget announcement](https://www.fairfaxcounty.gov/budget/board-supervisors-adopts-fy-27-budget-reduces-real-estate-tax-rate).
- The 2026 rate table lists the $1.12 base rate and parcel-specific district
  levies, including the $0.0325 stormwater service district. Not every listed
  rate applies to every parcel. Source: [Fairfax real-estate tax rates](https://www.fairfaxcounty.gov/taxes/node/199).
- The 2026 mean assessment was $1,012,504 for single-family homes, $612,580 for
  townhomes, and $387,560 for condominiums. Individual changes vary. Source:
  [2026 assessment notice release](https://www.fairfaxcounty.gov/taxes/real-estate/press-release-notices-sent).
- For tax year 2026, administrative assessment appeals were due April 1 and Board
  of Equalization appeals June 1. Taxes remained due during an appeal. Those dates
  are closed and must not be reused for 2027 without re-verification. Source:
  [Fairfax assessment appeals](https://www.fairfaxcounty.gov/taxes/real-estate/assessment-appeals).
- 2026 senior/disability relief has a $90,000 combined-income ceiling and $400,000
  combined net-asset ceiling, excluding the home and generally up to one acre.
  Relief tiers are 100%, 75%, 50%, and 25%. Source: [Fairfax tax relief](https://www.fairfaxcounty.gov/taxes/relief/tax-relief-seniors-people-with-disabilities).
