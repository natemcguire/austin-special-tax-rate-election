# Fairfax County real-estate tax: a resident's guide

Research draft — verified 2026-08-01. This is general information, not tax or
legal advice. Your assessment notice, tax district, and official bill control.

## How the bill works

Virginia requires real property to be assessed at fair market value. Fairfax's
simplified base-tax calculation is:

`assessed value × base rate ÷ 100 = base real-estate tax`

The adopted tax-year 2026 base rate is $1.12 per $100 of assessed value. Additional
district rates or service charges can apply to a parcel, so $1.12 is not always the
complete bill rate.

## Why a lower rate can still produce a higher bill

The Board lowered the base rate from $1.1225 to $1.12, but each bill also depends on
the property's new assessment. Fairfax reported 2026 mean assessments of $1,012,504
for single-family homes, $612,580 for townhomes, and $387,560 for condominiums.
Those are countywide category means, not predictions for a specific home.

Unlike a qualifying Texas homestead, Fairfax property has no general 10% annual
assessment cap. The legal standard is fair market value.

## Appeals and deadlines

An appeal should address fair-market value, uniformity, or an error in the property
description. Merely objecting to the size of the increase is not a legal basis.
For tax year 2026, the administrative deadline was April 1 and the Board of
Equalization deadline was June 1; both are now closed. Taxes remain due on time
during an appeal. Verify new dates each year with Fairfax County.

## Relief

For 2026, Fairfax's senior/disability real-estate relief program uses a $90,000
combined-income ceiling and a $400,000 net-asset ceiling, generally excluding the
home and up to one acre. Relief tiers range from 25% to 100%. Eligibility details,
documentation, deadlines, and the cap on property value receiving relief matter;
use the County application rather than this summary.

## Keep car tax separate

Virginia personal-property tax on vehicles is a different tax base and relief
system. Do not combine the vehicle rate or state “car tax” subsidy with the
real-estate calculation.
