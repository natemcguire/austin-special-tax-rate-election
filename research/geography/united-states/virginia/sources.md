# Virginia real-property-tax source record

Last verified: 2026-08-01.

- Virginia's Constitution requires assessments of real estate and tangible
  personal property at fair market value, subject to classifications and relief
  authorized by law. Source: [Virginia Constitution, Article X, Section 2](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/constitution/article10/section2/).
- Taxable real estate is segregated for local taxation. Source: [Virginia Code
  §58.1-3200](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/title58.1/chapter32/section58.1-3200/).
- A taxpayer aggrieved by a real-estate assessment may seek relief through the
  appropriate local assessment/Equalization process or court, subject to local
  procedure and statutory deadlines. Source: [Virginia Code, Chapter 32 Article
  12](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacodefull/title58.1/chapter32/article12/).
- Virginia does not impose Texas's 10% residence-homestead appraisal cap. Fairfax
  explicitly states there is no year-over-year percentage limit and that property
  must be assessed at fair market value. Source: [Fairfax real-estate FAQ](https://www.fairfaxcounty.gov/taxes/real-estate/frequently-asked-questions).
