# Texas property-tax source record

Last verified: 2026-08-01.

- Texas has no state property tax. Local appraisal districts determine taxable
  values and local taxing units adopt rates. Source: [Texas Comptroller Property
  Tax Assistance](https://comptroller.texas.gov/taxes/property-tax/).
- A qualifying residence homestead receives a mandatory $140,000 school-district
  exemption. A person age 65 or older or disabled receives an additional $60,000
  school-district exemption. Local units may adopt additional exemptions. Source:
  [Texas Comptroller exemptions](https://comptroller.texas.gov/taxes/property-tax/exemptions/?os=app).
- For an owner who qualified the same residence homestead in the preceding and
  current year, appraised value generally cannot rise more than 10% plus the
  market value of new improvements, and cannot exceed market value. This is an
  appraisal-value limit, not a limit on the tax bill. Source: [Texas Comptroller
  valuing property](https://comptroller.texas.gov/taxes/property-tax/valuing-property.php).
- The temporary non-homestead circuit breaker limits eligible real property
  valued at $5.32 million or less in tax year 2026 to a 20% annual appraised-value
  increase plus new improvements. It expires December 31, 2026 under current law.
  Source: [Texas Comptroller valuing property](https://comptroller.texas.gov/taxes/property-tax/valuing-property.php).
- No-new-revenue and voter-approval rates are calculated rates used in the annual
  truth-in-taxation process; neither is a promise that an individual bill will be
  unchanged. Source: [Texas Comptroller tax-rate calculations](https://comptroller.texas.gov/taxes/property-tax/truth-in-taxation/calculations.php)
  and [Texas Tax Code chapter 26](https://statutes.capitol.texas.gov/Docs/TX/pdf/TX.26.pdf).
- Deadlines depend on the event and property. Publish a calendar date only when it
  is tied to the current Comptroller calendar and the owner's appraisal notice.
  Source: [Property-tax law deadlines](https://comptroller.texas.gov/taxes/property-tax/calendars/deadlines.php).
