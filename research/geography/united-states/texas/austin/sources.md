# Austin source record

Last verified: 2026-08-01. Rates are dollars per $100 of taxable value unless
otherwise stated. “Proposed” figures must not be presented as adopted.

## FY 2026-27 proposal

- The City Manager presented a $6.6 billion proposed operating budget on July
  16, 2026. The proposed City property-tax rate is $0.57953, and the City's
  typical-home example estimates a $173.87 annual increase in the City portion
  of the property-tax bill. Budget adoption is scheduled for August 12–14.
  Source: [City news release](https://www.austintexas.gov/communications/news/austins-66-billion-proposed-budget-focused-fiscal-sustainability-enable-high).
- On July 30, Council approved a maximum proposed rate and set an August 12
  hearing. The action record expressly says this was not the final rate-setting
  action. Source: [Council action notes](https://services.austintexas.gov/council_meetings/action_notes.cfm?mid=1492), item 2.
- The City budget portal is the canonical location for the proposal volumes,
  taxpayer-impact statement, amendments, and later adopted documents. Source:
  [City budget portal](https://www.austintexas.gov/budget-excellence/city-budget).

## FY 2025-26 and Proposition Q

- Council originally approved $0.574017, five cents above the $0.524017
  voter-approval rate and contingent on the tax-rate election. Source:
  [City tax-rate record](https://www.austintexas.gov/budget-excellence/tax-rates).
- Proposition Q was not approved. The certified county components were:
  Travis 60,419 for / 104,256 against; Hays 35 / 91; Williamson 2,498 / 5,028.
  Combined: 62,952 for (36.53%) and 109,375 against (63.47%), 172,327 votes.
  Source: [City canvass backup](https://services.austintexas.gov/edims/document.cfm?id=462660), PDF pp. 2, 236 and 241.
- Council approved an amended $6.3 billion budget on November 20, 2025 after
  the proposition failed. Source: [City amended-budget release](https://www.austintexas.gov/communications/news/austin-city-council-approves-amended-fiscal-year-2025-2026-budget).

## Editorial cautions

- The City tax-rate page still displays the originally approved $0.574017 rate.
  Archive copy must explain that the election rejected the contingent five-cent
  increment and distinguish the original action from the effective ceiling.
- Citywide budget totals include enterprise funds and should not be compared
  directly with General Fund changes.
- Campaign pages are historical advocacy. Their estimates and characterizations
  are not neutral source material.
