# Local Tax Research

This is the shared, evidence-first research corpus for public tax explainers and
calculators. Austin/Texas and Fairfax County/Virginia are separate geographic
publishing tracks. Fairfax material is research-only until a dedicated domain is
selected and routed.

STORM is used to expand questions and discover sources. Its generated prose is
never treated as authoritative or published directly. Every public claim must be
verified against a primary source and entered in the evidence ledger with an
effective date.

## Editorial rules

- Separate adopted facts from proposals, estimates, campaign claims, and opinion.
- Prefer constitutions, statutes, tax authorities, adopted budgets, rate orders,
  appraisal districts, and certified election results.
- Include the taxing unit, tax year or fiscal year, valuation basis, exemptions,
  and units for every rate or dollar figure.
- Preserve campaign material as labeled history instead of rewriting it as current
  guidance.
- Recheck time-sensitive rates, exemptions, deadlines, elected officials, and
  pending legislation before publication.

## Structure

- `geography/united-states/federal/` — federal context shared by local projects.
- `geography/united-states/texas/` — Texas statewide sources.
- `geography/united-states/texas/austin/` — Austin sources, guide, evidence, and domains.
- `geography/united-states/virginia/` — Virginia statewide sources.
- `geography/united-states/virginia/fairfax/` — Fairfax sources, guide, evidence, and drafts.
- `campaigns/` — future campaign-specific source snapshots, organized beneath the
  relevant campaign slug and linked back to its geography.
- `people/` — future person-specific public-record dossiers; never mix these with
  general geographic reporting.
- `research-plan.md` — STORM topics, primary-source hierarchy, and publication gates.

Publishable assets follow the same geographic hierarchy under `public/geography/`.
Hostname routing hides the physical organization from canonical public URLs.
