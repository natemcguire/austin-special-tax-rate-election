Transparent calculator

Method and limits

Every input and rate is visible; no address or personal data is collected by the calculator.

Formula

Prior City levy = prior City taxable value × 0.524017 ÷ 100

Current City levy = current City taxable value × 0.579948 ÷ 100

Change = current levy − prior levy

What the inputs mean

Use the taxable value associated with the City of Austin line for each year. Do not substitute market value unless it is also the City's taxable value after the applicable exemption.

Boundaries

  • The $0.579948 rate was adopted August 12, 2026.
  • The result covers only the City property-tax levy.
  • It does not determine eligibility for an exemption or appraisal limitation.
  • It does not include escrow changes, penalties, interest, utilities, or fees.
  • Currency is displayed to cents after calculation; official tax systems control rounding.
