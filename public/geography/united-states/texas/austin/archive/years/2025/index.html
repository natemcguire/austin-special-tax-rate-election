What happened next

August 2025

Council adopts the original budget and calls the election

The rate included five cents above the voter-approval threshold.

November 4

Voters reject Proposition Q

The contingent increment could not take effect.

November 20

Council amends the budget

The City approved a $6.3 billion amended budget at the 52.40¢ voter-approval rate. That lasted about nine months.

August 12, 2026

Council adopts 57.99¢ anyway

The recalculated voter-approval rate came out above the 57.40¢ voters had rejected, so Council took it, 8–2, with no election. See the FY 2026–27 record.

November 3, 2026

Prop I on the ballot

An independent efficiency audit, in the charter, required before any future tax-rate election. This site asked for exactly that the morning after Prop Q lost. Vote YES. Read the case.
