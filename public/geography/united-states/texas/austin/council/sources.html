Methods

Show the record

The tracker separates evidence, interpretation, and historical campaign material.

Source order

  1. Ordinances, resolutions, approved minutes, vote records, and adopted budgets.
  2. Official rate worksheets, taxpayer-impact statements, contracts, audits, and datasets.
  3. Government announcements for chronology.
  4. Local reporting for context, clearly attributed.

Status vocabulary

Proposed

A staff or Manager recommendation that Council can change.

Approved

An item passed, but may still depend on later action or funding.

Adopted

The governing body's final budget or rate action for the cycle.

Certified

An election result after canvass.

Corrections

Every numeric correction should retain the source and verification date. Election-night totals are replaced by canvassed totals; proposed values are superseded rather than silently rewritten.

Research discovery uses the local STORM corpus, but no public claim is sourced to generated prose.
